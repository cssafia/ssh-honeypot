04 · Key term

Honeypot

A decoy system that looks real, but that nobody legitimate has any reason to use.

AnalogyA wallet left on a table, with a hidden camera. An honest person walks past. Whoever touches it is exactly who you wanted to see.
Any connection is suspicious
Every move is recorded
Nothing real is at risk
05 · Key term

Cowrie

A ready-made, open-source SSH honeypot. It records. It does not analyse.

  • Listens on port 2222 — the real SSH stays on 22
  • Gives attackers a fake shell: every answer is invented
  • Saves every action as a JSON log
  • Attackers never touch a real system
Not our code. SSH Sentinel is built on top of it.

08 · A fair question

Why a honeypot, not just a firewall?

Firewallthe locked door
BLOCKED
You learn: one log line
Honeypotthe decoy room with a camera
You learn: the attacker's whole playbook
Almost no false alarmsnobody legitimate uses it
Sees what happens after loginevery command typed
Safe to studya fake shell, nothing real
Early warningcheck the real servers
They work together. A honeypot sees only attacks aimed at it — it complements real defences, it does not replace them.
